# Demand side and market: paying Argentine merchant QR with USDC (foreign travelers and international freelancers)

Research date: 2026-11-10. Every figure has a date. "Inference" means a conclusion drawn by the researcher, not a sourced fact.

## 1. Size of the target segments (tourist arrivals, origin, spend, stay, digital nomads)

### Takeaway
Argentina gets about 2.25M foreign tourists a year through the Buenos Aires airports (Ezeiza and Aeroparque), who spent about US$2.7B in 2025. Arrivals fell in 2025 because Argentina had become expensive in dollar terms. They recovered in 2026: Q2 2026 air arrivals were up about 22% year on year, and long-haul origins (US/Canada, Europe) are growing fastest. Brazil is the largest single origin, at 27–31% of tourists. Figures on digital nomads are weak, roughly 11k–22k in Buenos Aires.

### Cited Findings
- **2025 full year, Ezeiza + Aeroparque, inbound:** 2,251.1 thousand non-resident tourists (-10.8% YoY); total spend about US$2,687.0M; 31.7M overnight stays (-6.4%). Source: INDEC ETI, annex tables 17–18 of the June 2026 report — [INDEC ETI junio 2026 (PDF)](https://www.indec.gob.ar/uploads/informesdeprensa/eti_07_2611F2D28020.pdf). The units come from the column headers ("Miles de turistas", "Miles de USD"). The PDF layout is messy, so the spend year-on-year change could not be read reliably.
- **Q2 2026, Ezeiza + Aeroparque, inbound:** 562.5k tourists (+21.8% YoY), US$608.8M total spend, 12.4 nights average stay (-8.6%), US$87.3 average daily spend per person. Same [INDEC ETI junio 2026](https://www.indec.gob.ar/uploads/informesdeprensa/eti_07_2611F2D28020.pdf).
  - Brazil: 152.5k (+19.4%), 6.9 nights, US$112.6/day, 27.1% of air tourists.
  - US & Canada: 77.0k (+33.8%), 14.8 nights, US$97.9/day.
  - Europe: 99.6k (+11.7%), 21.5 nights, US$65.6/day.
- **June 2026, all routes (air, land, river):** 329.2k non-resident tourists (+3.3% YoY). 69.2% came from bordering countries: Brazil 30.8% (101.4k, +15.2%), Uruguay 14.2% (46.9k, -27.6%), Chile 12.8% (42.3k, +18.5%). US/Canada had 25.2k (+16.5%) and Europe 38.2k (+8.6%). 56.5% arrived by air. Source: [INDEC ETI junio 2026](https://www.indec.gob.ar/uploads/informesdeprensa/eti_07_2611F2D28020.pdf).
- **H1 2026:** more than 3.1M foreign tourists. Visitors from non-bordering countries reached 1,434,001, a 25-year record; the top source markets were the US, Spain and Peru. This is reported via DolarHoy/Global66, not checked directly against the official source — [DolarHoy](https://dolarhoy.com/nota/los-turistas-extranjeros-ya-pagan-con-qr-en-argentina-el-volumen-supero-los-usd-1-7-millones-20268616400).
- **Jan–Sep 2026:** 4,229,200 international tourists (+8.5% vs the same period of 2025), per a secondary search snippet citing the Ministerio de Turismo. Not verified against the primary source — [Promociones Aéreas / search aggregate](https://promociones-aereas.com.ar/noticias/turismo-internacional-en-argentina-record-de-visitantes-y-fuerte-recuperacion-en-2025).
- **2025 monthly declines (all routes):** March 479.6k (-24.0%), July 427.2k (-16.0%), September 374.8k (-18.9%). November 2025: 491.4k tourists, with Europe +4.7%. Sources: [INDEC ETI marzo 2025](https://www.indec.gob.ar/uploads/informesdeprensa/eti_04_257F79FE4B90.pdf), [INDEC ETI noviembre 2025](https://www.indec.gob.ar/uploads/informesdeprensa/eti_12_254823CF51B2.pdf). Industry press attributes the 2025 decline to peso appreciation making Argentina expensive — [Turismo Cero](https://turismocero.com/entrevistas/turismo-en-argentina-en-2025-auge-del-emisivo-y-seniales-de-alerta-en-el-receptivo.htm).
- **Spend per day, late 2025 (ETI air):** November 2025 average US$93.9/day, with Brazilians highest at US$122.3/day. August 2025: Brazil US$117.0/day, Chile US$62.0/day. Sources: [INDEC ETI nov 2025](https://www.indec.gob.ar/uploads/informesdeprensa/eti_12_254823CF51B2.pdf), [INDEC ETI ago 2025](https://www.indec.gob.ar/uploads/informesdeprensa/eti_09_25BED08EC2DC.pdf).
- **Accommodation, Q2 2026 inbound air:** 26.4% stayed in rented houses or apartments (US$109.2/day), a proxy for longer-stay, nomad-like visitors — [INDEC ETI junio 2026](https://www.indec.gob.ar/uploads/informesdeprensa/eti_07_2611F2D28020.pdf).
- **Digital nomads:**
  - An unsourced estimate of "more than 11,220 digital nomads in Buenos Aires", mainly from the US, Brazil and France (Sept 2025) — [Chaxxel](https://chaxxel.com.ar/buenos-aires-rompe-records-la-ciudad-preferida-por-nomades-digitales-en-latinoamerica/).
  - The Buenos Aires City "Digital Nomads BA" program targets 22,000 nomads by 2026 (same source).
  - The digital nomad visa (from 2022) lasts 180 days plus one 180-day extension — [Golden Harbors](https://goldenharbors.com/articles/argentina-digital-nomad-visa).

### Inferences
- Air arrivals spend about US$1,100–1,200 per trip. Q2 2026: 608.8M / 562.5k ≈ US$1,082. 2025: 2,687M / 2.251M ≈ US$1,194. A US$30 average QR payment means a tourist would make about 35–40 payments per trip if all spend went through QR. In practice, hotels, flights and tours are paid by card or prepaid, so QR-addressable spend is probably 20–40% of the total (assumption).
- Rough serviceable market: airport tourists × 30% QR-addressable share ≈ US$800M/yr of QR-addressable spend. At a 1% fee and 70% revenue share, each 1% captured is about US$56k/yr of net revenue. This is an illustrative calculation, not a forecast.
- Long-stay segments (Europe 21.5 nights, US/Canada 14.8 nights, apartment renters) give the most transactions per user. They are also the most likely to already hold USDC. Brazilians are numerous but stay short (6.9 nights) and have a Pix substitute (see section 5).

### Gaps
- Could not read the official 2025 all-routes annual total (only the airport subtotal) or the 2026 monthly series after June directly from INDEC.
- No official count of digital nomads, remote workers or foreign residents (Migraciones or the city program).
- No data found on foreign freelancers living in Argentina who are paid in USDC (Deel, Payoneer, Bitwage reports were not reached within the search budget).

## 2. How tourists pay today and what it costs; has the crypto arbitrage shrunk?

### Takeaway
The old "blue dollar" arbitrage is largely gone. In 2026 the official–MEP gap is about 3–6%, and foreign cards settle near the official or financial rate. A residual spread remains: per one competitor's October 2026 snapshot, crypto apps' implied rates were about 6–10% better than the foreign-card rate. The reason to use the product is moving from saving money on FX to convenience and access: paying QR-only merchants without a DNI and avoiding cash and card dynamic currency conversion (DCC). That spread is a dated, competitor-sourced claim and could disappear.

### Cited Findings
- **April 2025 cepo exit:** the BCRA removed purchase limits for individuals. The 30% tax withholding (percepción) on card spending in foreign currency stayed (this applies to residents' spending abroad) — [Infobae, 11 Apr 2025](https://www.infobae.com/economia/2025/04/11/fin-del-cepo-que-va-a-pasar-con-el-dolar-ahorro-el-dolar-turista-y-las-compras-con-tarjeta/).
- **Exchange-rate gap:** since early 2026 the official–MEP gap has been about 3–6% — [Plato, Apr 2026](https://plato.com.ar/blog/comprar-propiedad-dolar-mep-post-cepo/).
- **Further 2026 easing:** the cross-restriction between MEP/CCL and the official market was relaxed from 14 Apr 2026 (Comunicación "A" 8127) — [CambioDolarHoy, May 2026](https://cambiodolarhoy.com/blog/cepo-resumen-mayo/). A low-reliability site claims the cepo fully ended on 14 Aug 2026 — [Statuncore](https://statuncore.com/2026/08/14/14-de-agosto-2026-07-48hsdolar-oficial-y-dolar-tarjeta-banco-por-banco-a-cuanto-/). Unverified and contradicted by other sources.
- **Earlier regime (pre-2025):** the BCRA granted foreign cards, and later wallets used by tourists, a MEP-like rate. This made the "dólar tarjeta/MEP for tourists" relevant then — [Perfil](https://www.perfil.com/noticias/economia/los-turistas-extranjeros-podran-pagar-con-billeteras-virtuales-al-dolar-mep.phtml), [El Cronista "Dólar QR"](https://www.cronista.com/finanzas-mercados/dolar-qr-turistas-que-usen-billeteras-virtuales-tendran-un-tipo-de-cambio-similar-a-los-financieros/). With the gap at 3–6%, this distinction now matters little (inference).
- **Rate snapshot, October 2026 (CacaoCash, an interested party):**
  - Foreign-card (Fiserv) rate about ARS 1,465/USD; "crypto dollar" about ARS 1,598/USD, about 9% better before fees.
  - After CacaoCash's 2.5% fee, about 1,559, i.e. 6–10% better than a card.
  - An August 2026 test reportedly got 1,509 via a QR app vs 1,378 via Visa.
  - Source: [CacaoCash guide 2026](https://cacaocash.com/guides/best-apps-pay-qr-argentina-foreigners-2026).
- **Card risks for tourists:** Wise warns that dynamic currency conversion can lock in worse rates and recommends paying in pesos — [Wise AR](https://wise.com/ar/blog/como-pagar-en-argentina-siendo-extranjero). Under the earlier regime, ATM cash withdrawals did not get the preferential rate — [Ser Viajera 2025](https://www.ser-viajera.com/como-conviene-pagar-en-argentina/).
- **Tourists cannot open the dominant local wallets:** Mercado Pago, Ualá and Lemon require a DNI/CUIL; belo and Prex reportedly require LatAm or Argentine residency (unverified) — [CacaoCash guide](https://cacaocash.com/guides/best-apps-pay-qr-argentina-foreigners-2026).

### Inferences
- The "crypto beats official FX by 20–100%" story from 2019–2024 is over. Any remaining spread (about 5–9% in late 2026 per one source) comes from the crypto/MEP vs card-network rate and could close quickly. A pitch built on FX savings is fragile; a pitch built on access (pay QR-only merchants without a DNI or cash) and transparency is sturdier.
- A 1% fee looks competitive against stated competitor fees of 1–2.5% plus spread (see section 7).

### Gaps
- No official BCRA or ARCA confirmation of the exact rate applied to non-resident cards in late 2026, or whether the 30% percepción was abolished in August 2026.
- No survey of tourist payment mix (card, cash, QR, Western Union) for 2025–2026. Western Union and Wise/Revolut usage among tourists in Argentina was not quantified.

## 3. QR payments penetration in Argentina

### Takeaway
Interoperable QR is mass-market. Transfer-based payments started via QR (PCT) ran at about 58–78M a month in 2025, and a later BCRA figure cited in press puts them at 102.5M a month. More than 700k merchants had QR years ago. QR fees for merchants (about 0.6–0.8% from account balance) are well below card POS fees (debit about 3.25% on MP Point). Merchants can legally take QR instead of debit cards, which creates "QR-only" merchants that tourists with foreign cards cannot pay.

### Cited Findings
- **QR-initiated transfer payments (PCT), BCRA Informe de Pagos Minoristas, 2025:**
  - Feb: 58.4M operations
  - Mar: 72.3M
  - Apr: 70.4M
  - May: 63.3M
  - Jul: 67.0M
  - Aug: 69.9M
  - Oct: 77.5M (the November report revises October to 75.8M)
  - Sources: [Feb](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/en/publicaciones/retail-payments-report-february-2025/), [Mar](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/en/publicaciones/retail-payments-report-march-2025/), [Apr](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/en/publicaciones/retail-payments-report-april-2025/), [May](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/en/publicaciones/retail-payments-report-may-2025/), [Jul](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/en/publicaciones/retail-payments-report-july-2025/), [Aug](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/en/publicaciones/retail-payments-report-august-2025/), [Oct](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/en/publicaciones/retail-payments-monthly-report-october-2025/), [Nov](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/en/publicaciones/monthly-report-on-retail-payments-november-2025/).
- **Later BCRA figure (via press):** "QR represent 98.8% of transfers [PCT]; 102.5M operations for about ARS 2.4 trillion" — [DolarHoy citing BCRA, 2026](https://dolarhoy.com/nota/los-turistas-extranjeros-ya-pagan-con-qr-en-argentina-el-volumen-supero-los-usd-1-7-millones-20268616400). The month it refers to is not stated in the article. ARS 2.4 trillion / 102.5M ≈ ARS 23k average ticket, about US$15–16 at about ARS 1,465–1,600 (inference).
- **Merchant base:** more than 700k merchants had QR (Cámara Argentina Fintech; old publication date, likely outdated) — [Cámara Fintech](https://camarafintech.org/tips-para-cobrar-y-pagar-con-qr-para-comercios-y-consumidores/).
- **Legal rule:** a merchant that accepts QR is not obliged to accept debit cards — [Argentina.gob.ar Ley simple](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/justicia/derechofacil/leysimple/pagos-con-tarjetas-de-debito).
- **Merchant fees (non-official comparators, 2026):**
  - Mercado Pago QR 0.8% to about 6.29% + IVA, depending on how the buyer pays; MP Point debit 3.25% + IVA.
  - MODO about 0.8%; Banco Provincia QR 0.6–0.8% + IVA.
  - Source: [jonatanalmeira.com](https://www.jonatanalmeira.com/como-cobrar-con-qr-argentina/). DolarHoy says QR is cheaper for merchants than cards — [DolarHoy](https://dolarhoy.com/economia/el-boom-del-qr-en-latam-con-argentina-como-protagonista-menos-costos-para-comercios-y-personas-frente-al-pago-con-tarjetas-20268208380).
- Transferencias 3.0 interoperable QR first passed 50k daily transactions in Dec 2021 — [BCRA](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/en/news/3-0-transfers-over-50-000-daily-transactions-already-carried-out/).

### Inferences
- Acceptance is not the bottleneck: interoperable QR is close to universal in urban retail. The pain point is that foreigners cannot hold an Argentine wallet. The product removes a KYC/residency barrier rather than creating acceptance.
- Small merchants' preference for QR (0.6–0.8% vs about 3% card POS) means some cafés, kiosks and ferias are effectively QR/cash only. This is the clearest use case.

### Gaps
- No current official count of QR-accepting merchants; no split between Mercado Pago, MODO and other acceptors.
- No data on what share of merchants refuse cards outright.

## 4. Stablecoin/crypto adoption among Argentines and foreigners; freelancers paid in USDC

### Takeaway
Argentina is LatAm's #2 crypto market: US$88.5B in Jul 2025–Jun 2026, +15.3%. Stablecoins make up more than half of local-currency exchange purchases. Local crypto-to-QR spending is already established through Binance Pay QR, Lemon and belo, but those require local ID. The foreigner-specific USDC segment is real but small and unmeasured.

### Cited Findings
- **Chainalysis 2026 (Jul 2025–Jun 2026):** Argentina had US$88.5B in crypto activity, second in LatAm after Brazil, growing 15.3% vs 9.8% for the region. Wallets holding at least US$10k in stablecoins grew 82% since Jul 2024 — [Chainalysis LatAm 2026](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/latin-america-crypto-adoption-2026/).
- **Chainalysis 2025:** US$93.9B received Jul 2022–Jun 2025 (#2 in LatAm). Stablecoins were more than half of local-currency exchange purchases in Argentina and Brazil (Jul 2024–Jun 2025) — [Chainalysis LatAm 2025](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/latin-america-crypto-adoption-2025/). Sources disagree on the global index rank (15th vs 20th).
- **USDC vs USDT:** USDC is less liquid than USDT in ARS peer-to-peer markets; low-fee USDC usage relies on Solana or Base — [Spark research](https://www.spark.money/research/stablecoin-argentina-peso-crisis-adoption).
- **Local crypto-to-QR products (resident-only or restricted):**
  - Binance Pay QR in Argentina (USDT, BTC and 100+ assets, auto-converted to ARS; Argentina users only) — [El Cronista](https://www.cronista.com/infotechnology/finanzas-digitales/el-gigante-cripto-binance-lanzo-pagos-con-qr-en-argentina-como-funcionan/).
  - Lemon QR (USDT/USDC/DAI/ETH/BTC; 1% cashback when paying with crypto) — [Lemon](https://lemon.me/en/qr), [La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/economia/negocios/pagos-digitales-como-recibir-dinero-por-cada-compra-realizada-con-qr-nid08082025/).
  - belo QR in ARS or USDT/USDC — [belo](https://www.belo.app/en-us/blog/pagar-con-qr-belo-argentina).

### Inferences
- Argentines already have USDC-to-QR. Any wedge has to be non-residents, for whom KYC with only a passport is the differentiator.
- USDC is the minority stablecoin locally, so the licensed provider's USDC→ARS liquidity and spread need checking. USDT support could matter even for foreigners.

### Gaps
- No Chainalysis or other data on crypto use by foreigners in Argentina.
- No Deel, Payoneer or Bitwage figures for USDC-paid freelancers in or visiting Argentina (not reached within budget). This is the biggest open question for the freelancer segment.

## 5. Brazilian tourists: Pix as substitute

### Takeaway
Pix is a strong substitute for Brazilians. Since February 2025, Mercado Pago merchants with Point Smart terminals can take Pix from any Brazilian wallet via QR. Brazilians are the largest group (27–31% of tourists) but stay only about 7 nights. They are a poor first segment for a USDC product.

### Cited Findings
- **Mercado Pago Pix acceptance (Feb 2025):** Mercado Pago merchants can charge Brazilian tourists with Pix. The merchant selects Pix on the Point Smart terminal, a QR is generated, and the buyer pays from any Brazilian bank or wallet app — [Mercado Libre newsroom](https://news.mercadolibre.com/cobrar-con-mercado-pago-pix-a-brasilenos), [Infobae/EFE, 4 Feb 2025](https://www.infobae.com/america/agencias/2025/02/04/mercado-libre-permitira-el-pago-por-el-sistema-pix-a-turistas-brasilenos-en-argentina/), [La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/economia/negocios/explosion-pix-mercado-pago-sumo-una-funcion-que-permite-cobrarles-a-brasilenos-con-codigo-qr-nid04022025/).
- Brazil was 22% of Argentina's tourists in 2024, per government data cited by Mercado Pago — [Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/america/agencias/2025/02/04/mercado-libre-permitira-el-pago-por-el-sistema-pix-a-turistas-brasilenos-en-argentina/).
- Brazil was 30.8% of all-route tourists in June 2026 and 27.1% of Q2 2026 air tourists, staying 6.9 nights at US$112.6/day — [INDEC ETI junio 2026](https://www.indec.gob.ar/uploads/informesdeprensa/eti_07_2611F2D28020.pdf).
- CacaoCash lists Brazil Pix as a funding rail for its foreigner QR app — [CacaoCash](https://cacaocash.com/guides/best-apps-pay-qr-argentina-foreigners-2026).

### Inferences
- The Pix route only works at Mercado Pago merchants with Point Smart terminals, not every interoperable QR. A gap remains at MODO/bank QR merchants and at sticker-QR merchants without a terminal. Brazilians still have a near-zero-friction fiat option for most of their spend, so USDC is not needed.

### Gaps
- No usage volume for Pix-in-Argentina, and no data on whether Pix works with printed or static QR codes.
- Did not verify Nubank or Pix Internacional (cross-border) options for Argentine QR.

## 6. Comparable markets and precedents

### Takeaway
Governments and wallets are building crypto-to-local-QR rails for tourists: Thailand's TouristDigiPay sandbox (from Q4 2025) and Bitget Wallet on VietQR (June 2025, 2M+ merchants). Public usage data is almost nonexistent and most claims are promotional. The pattern is consistent: crypto is converted by a licensed entity and the merchant gets local fiat via national QR. That is the same architecture as this startup.

### Cited Findings
- **Thailand TouristDigiPay:** announced by the Thai SEC on 18 Aug 2025 as an 18-month sandbox from Q4 2025. Tourists open a licensed digital-asset exchange account plus a licensed e-money wallet, convert crypto to baht and pay by QR, with no cash withdrawal. Aim: revive falling arrivals — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/thailand-plans-launch-crypto-payment-tourists), [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/digital-assets/40054185), [Siam Legal](https://www.siam-legal.com/thailand-law/thailand-launches-crypto-to-baht-touristdigipay-sandbox/). A reported 500k THB/month cap is unconfirmed — [Kursiv](https://uz.kursiv.media/en/2025-08-18/thailand-launches-18-month-crypto-to-baht-sandbox-to-revive-tourism/).
- **Bitget Wallet × VietQR (June 2025):** stablecoin payments at more than 2M merchants via licensed partner AEON; 50% cashback promo for the first 50k new users; Philippines and Brazil announced as next — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/06/18/3101152/0/en/Bitget-Wallet-Expands-Crypto-Payment-Capabilities-with-National-QR-Integration.html), [Bitget blog](https://web3.bitget.com/en/blog/articles/national-qr-code-payment-vn-ph).

### Inferences
- Bitget's 50% cashback suggests that getting crypto users to switch to QR spending needed heavy subsidy. Organic demand may be thin.
- Thailand's need for a state sandbox shows the regulatory path matters. In Argentina the licensed-PSP-plus-PSAV route already exists, which is an advantage.

### Gaps
- No usage or volume data for TouristDigiPay, Bitget VietQR, or crypto-to-QR in Mexico, Turkey or Bali (Bali has explicitly banned crypto payments, but this was not verified in this pass).

## 7. Willingness to pay and competitor traction

### Takeaway
At least five foreigner-oriented apps already pay Argentine QR, at stated costs from about 1% to 2.5% plus spread. Public traction data is tiny: P2P.me Argentina at about US$86k/month and 108 MAU; Global66 tourist QR at US$1.7M over 10 months. Users accept about 1–3% total cost. Demand exists but is currently small in absolute volume.

### Cited Findings
- **Competitors serving foreigners with passport-only KYC (as of Sept–Oct 2026; source is a competitor):**
  - CacaoCash: 2.5% per payment; funded by USDC, ACH, SEPA, Pix, etc.
  - WanderWallet: US$0.20 + 1% per payment plus deposit fees; funded by USDC on Polygon, ACH, SEPA, Revolut Pay.
  - Moreta Pay: 1.5% per QR plus about 0.6–0.75% spread; funded by USDC/USDT on Solana, cards at 2.5%.
  - belo: about 1.3% spread, residency possibly required.
  - Source: [CacaoCash guide](https://cacaocash.com/guides/best-apps-pay-qr-argentina-foreigners-2026).
- **Global66 (Oct 2025–Jul 2026):** foreign tourists made more than US$1.7M of QR payments, 67.4% of the platform's QR volume (61–72% in 2026). Average foreign ticket US$21.88 vs US$20.53 overall. Chile was more than 50% of total volume (US$1.3M+, ticket US$23.94). July 2026 transactions were double January's — [DolarHoy](https://dolarhoy.com/nota/los-turistas-extranjeros-ya-pagan-con-qr-en-argentina-el-volumen-supero-los-usd-1-7-millones-20268616400), [TotalMedios](https://www.totalmedios.com/nota/64788/los-turistas-extranjeros-ya-representan-el-67-del-volumen-de-pagos-con-qr-de-global66).
- **P2P.me:** the operator dashboard shows Argentina at about US$86,195 monthly volume, 363 monthly orders and 108 MAU (live snapshot, undated) — [ops.p2p.me](https://ops.p2p.me/). Global claims are inconsistent: US$4.5M / 5.5k users on [p2p.me](https://p2p.me/en) vs US$31.4M / 22.7k users on [IQ.wiki](https://iq.wiki/wiki/p2pme).
- Yodl markets stablecoins-to-pesos for Argentina — [Yodl](https://yodl.me/argentina/stablecoins-to-pesos/). No volume found.

### Inferences
- **Ticket size:** Global66 tourist tickets are about US$22, and BCRA's overall QR average is about US$15–16. The US$30 assumption looks high. At US$22, revenue per payment is about US$0.15, not US$0.21.
- **P2P.me's average order** is about US$237 (86k/363). That looks like top-ups or larger transfers rather than café payments; ticket behaviour varies by product.
- A 1% fee with no spread would undercut most stated competitors, but it leaves little room for subsidy. The economics need volume: at US$0.15–0.21 per payment, US$10k/month net revenue takes about 50–65k payments a month.

### Gaps
- No user surveys or Reddit evidence collected on the fees travelers accept (Reddit threads not searched within budget).
- No Bitget Wallet Argentina data, and no independent check of any competitor's volume.

## 8. Most promising starting sub-segment and acquisition channels

### Takeaway
The best first segment looks like long-stay, crypto-native foreigners in Buenos Aires: digital nomads, remote freelancers paid in USDC, and crypto-event attendees from the US and Europe. They stay 15–21+ nights, many rent apartments, they cannot open local wallets and they already hold USDC. Brazilians (who have Pix) and Chileans (served by Global66-style fiat fintechs) are the wrong place to start.

### Cited Findings
- **Stay and growth, Q2 2026 air:** Europe 21.5 nights (+11.7% arrivals), US/Canada 14.8 nights (+33.8%) vs Brazil 6.9 nights; 26.4% of inbound air tourists rented a house or apartment — [INDEC ETI junio 2026](https://www.indec.gob.ar/uploads/informesdeprensa/eti_07_2611F2D28020.pdf).
- **Digital nomads:** about 11k (unsourced), with a city goal of 22k by 2026 — [Chaxxel](https://chaxxel.com.ar/buenos-aires-rompe-records-la-ciudad-preferida-por-nomades-digitales-en-latinoamerica/).
- **Chileans** lead fiat-funded tourist QR use (more than 50% of Global66 volume) — [DolarHoy](https://dolarhoy.com/nota/los-turistas-extranjeros-ya-pagan-con-qr-en-argentina-el-volumen-supero-los-usd-1-7-millones-20268616400). This shows demand for QR access exists without crypto.

### Inferences
- **Suggested beachhead:** crypto-event attendees (Devconnect/Ethereum Argentina-type events) for launch bursts and testimonials, then retention among Palermo/Recoleta nomads via coworkings, nomad Slack/WhatsApp groups, and short-term rental hosts. Events give a dense pool of USDC-holding users in one week; nomads give repeated transactions over months.
- **Alternative wedge:** support fiat on-ramps (ACH/SEPA/Pix) like the competitors do, to reach the non-crypto Chilean, US and European tourist majority. That turns the product into a general "foreigner QR wallet" competing with Global66, CacaoCash, WanderWallet and Moreta.
- **Main demand risk:** the segment where USDC-native is a real advantage (crypto-native foreigners) is probably in the low tens of thousands of people a year. Several competitors already target it, so differentiation (price, UX, wallet-native EVM flow with no account) matters more than market size.
